The name **"Chamlak" (ចម្លាក់)** is rooted in the classical Khmer art of stone and wood carving—a discipline demanding supreme precision, cultural devotion, and artistic mastery. In the digital era, **CHAMLAK** modernizes this heritage by carving raw technology into sophisticated digital solutions for the Kingdom of Cambodia.

## 2. Executive Summary

**CHAMLAK** is a pioneer Cambodian technology, media, and entertainment group headquartered in Phnom Penh. Operating through its centralized domain **chamlak.com**, the enterprise unites four key pillars:

1. **CHAMLAK AI** — Advanced localized artificial intelligence.
2. **CHAMLAK MEDIA** — Core media engine and operational distribution hub.
3. **CHAMLAK MUSIC** — Audio engineering, music production, and software plugin development.
4. **CHAMLAK MOVIE** — Film industry production and Khmer-language video streaming platform.

By synthesizing technical innovations—such as voice sentiment recognition, custom audio software plugins, localized language modeling, and native streaming platforms—CHAMLAK elevates Cambodian human potential and safeguards digital cultural heritage.

## 3. Vision & Mission

### 🌟 Vision
To build Cambodia’s definitive AI-driven technology and media ecosystem at **chamlak.com**, preserving the Khmer language, ethics, and legal knowledge while broadcasting Cambodian artistry and technical excellence worldwide.

### 🎯 Mission
* **Empower Khmer Digital Identity:** Develop native AI models strictly compliant with the **Chuon Nath Dictionary** standard across all conversational registers (Royal, Monk, formal, and general speech).
* **Provide Intelligent Local Solutions:** Deliver rapid, empathetic AI responses using voice and sentiment analysis to solve everyday problems for Cambodian citizens.
* **Democratize Knowledge:** Provide accessible legal education, civic ethics, and digital literacy tools across the population without discrimination.
* **Innovate Media & Distribution:** Combine surround sound engineering, custom software plugins, cinematic film production, and local-language streaming apps under one roof.

## 5. Division Breakdown

### 🤖 1. CHAMLAK AI — *"The Brain of Khmer People"*
The technological nucleus of the entire ecosystem.
* **100% Khmer Language Orthography:** Strictly integrated with the standard Samdech Preah Sangha Raja Chuon Nath Dictionary, dynamically adjusting across Royal Register (*Raja-sap*), Monk Register (*Sangha-sap*), and general conversation.
* **Voice & Sentiment Recognition:** Real-time acoustic analysis that detects speaker gender, age group, and emotional state to deliver humanized, empathetic interaction.
* **Specialized Persona Agents:**
  * **Lok Kru Socheat (លោកគ្រូ សុជាតិ):** AI Legal & Civic Rights Advisor providing clear, accessible legal guidance.
  * **Cultural & Historical Guides:** Interactive digital archives of Khmer history and traditional arts.
  * **Educational Tutors:** Youth-focused companions promoting digital literacy.
* **Privacy & Legal Safety:** Engineered strictly in alignment with the laws, data privacy standards, and cultural ethics of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

---

### 📺 2. CHAMLAK MEDIA — *"The Operational Core"*
The central operational engine managing brand integrity, content syndication, operational execution, and digital media deployment across all branches under **chamlak.com**.

---

### 🎵 3. CHAMLAK MUSIC — *"Audio Engineering & DSP Innovation"*
A music-technology hub bridging traditional Khmer acoustics with modern digital signal processing (DSP).
* **Proprietary Audio Plugins:** Custom DSP software and audio plugin tools developed by Mr. Morng Chuk for global producers, sound engineers, and content creators.
* **High-Fidelity Production:** Full-scale music composition, custom film scoring, and spatial mastering for local creators.

---

### 🎬 4. CHAMLAK MOVIE — *"Film Industry & Khmer Streaming App"*
A full-pipeline film sector dedicated to producing and delivering high-quality cinema directly to Cambodian households.
* **Dedicated Movie App (Primary Project Focus):** Developing a native streaming app allowing Cambodian audiences to watch cinema seamlessly in their local language.
* **Cinematic Surround Sound:** Advanced multi-channel theatrical and mobile spatial audio design tailored for modern viewing environments.
* **End-to-End Production:** Complete film production pipeline directed by Mr. Morng Chuk, integrating traditional storytelling with AI-driven production tools.

## 6. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)

Across **chamlak.com**, CHAMLAK commits to:
* **Preserving Intangible Cultural Heritage:** Digitizing traditional Khmer speech etiquette, literature, and folklore to ensure ancient culture thrives in the age of generative tech.
* **Closing the Digital Divide:** Providing accessible AI tools, localized movie streaming, and legal education to communities across Cambodia.
* **Empowering Creators:** Building tools, software plugins, and distribution platforms that empower local Cambodian artists, programmers, and filmmakers.
