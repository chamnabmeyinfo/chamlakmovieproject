# CHAMLAK MOVIE

**Cambodia's premium streaming app for films & short dramas — fully voiced over in high-quality Khmer.**

Chamlak Movie is the **film & streaming division of CHAMLAK** ([chamlak.com](https://chamlak.com)).
It licenses and produces films and short-form dramas, creates professional Khmer-language
voice-over versions and original productions with cinematic surround sound, and distributes
them to viewers in Cambodia through a mobile app on a subscription and pay-per-view (credit) basis.

> **Status:** Early-stage project (pre-incorporation).
> **Founders:** Mr. MORNG CHUK (CCTO) and Mr. CHAMNAB MEY (CEO).

---

## Part of the CHAMLAK group

CHAMLAK (ចម្លាក់) is a Cambodian technology, media and entertainment group operating at
**chamlak.com**, built on four pillars:

| Pillar | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| 🤖 **CHAMLAK AI** | Khmer-first AI — language, voice & sentiment, civic/legal guidance. |
| 📺 **CHAMLAK MEDIA** | Core media engine — brand, content syndication and distribution. |
| 🎵 **CHAMLAK MUSIC** | Audio engineering, film scoring, surround sound & DSP plugins. |
| 🎬 **CHAMLAK MOVIE** | Film production & Khmer-language streaming app *(this project)*. |

Chamlak Movie draws on the wider group — in-house surround sound and scoring (CHAMLAK MUSIC),
AI localization and production tools (CHAMLAK AI), and distribution (CHAMLAK MEDIA).

## Business model

- **Content:** Licensed and originally produced films and short dramas, re-voiced in Khmer with cinematic surround sound.
- **Distribution:** App-exclusive — full titles are never posted on social media.
- **Revenue:** VIP subscriptions (weekly / monthly / annual, plus Pro & Plus tiers) and a credit (coin) model, paid via KHQR, ABA, Wing and card.
