## Recommended display order

For higher conversion, display the plans in this order:

1. Monthly VIP — Most Popular
2. Annual VIP — Best Value
3. 7-Day Starter — Low-risk trial
4. Weekly VIP — Short-term option
5. VIP Pro — Multi-device option
6. VIP Plus — Family or premium option

If your current grid must remain six cards, highlight Monthly VIP and Annual VIP visually and keep the other plans less prominent.

---

## Short benefit labels

Use simple language on the cards:

- **1 ឧបករណ៍** — 1 device
- **2 ឧបករណ៍** — 2 devices
- **4 ឧបករណ៍** — 4 devices
- **មាតិកា VIP** — VIP content
- **មានអក្សររត់** — Subtitles available
- **ការមើលមានសុវត្ថិភាព** — Secure viewing
- **មាតិកា Premium** — Premium content, only if available

Avoid listing technical features such as DRM or anti-screen capture as the main selling point. Customers decide based on access, value, quality, and convenience.

- [ ] Annual VIP is labelled **Best Value**.
- [ ] Every plan has one clear reason to choose it.
- [ ] Device limits are accurate and easy to understand.
- [ ] VIP Pro and VIP Plus have different real benefits.
- [ ] Renewal, cancellation, and refund terms are visible.
- [ ] KHQR, ABA, Wing, and card options are shown only if supported.
- [ ] Khmer text is readable on mobile screens.
- [ ] All subtitles, quality, security, and premium-content claims are accurate.
- [ ] Movie and drama content is properly licensed for distribution.

## Final recommendation

Make **Monthly VIP** the easiest choice for regular viewers and **Annual VIP** the smartest value. Keep the trial as the low-risk entry point, but simplify the card text so visitors can decide in a few seconds.
