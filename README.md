# Chamlak Movie

**Cambodia's premium streaming app for films & short dramas — fully voiced over in high-quality Khmer.**

Chamlak Movie licenses films and short-form dramas from suppliers worldwide, produces
professional Khmer-language voice-over versions, and distributes them to viewers in
Cambodia through a mobile app on a subscription and pay-per-view (credit) basis.

> **Status:** Early-stage project (pre-incorporation). Founders: AEK and CHAMNAB.

## Business model

- **Content:** Licensed films and short dramas, re-voiced in Khmer.
- **Distribution:** App-exclusive — full titles are never posted on social media.
- **Revenue:** Weekly / monthly subscriptions plus a credit (coin) model, paid via local wallets.
